Revision B gasket stack-up

Nominal compression 25.0%. Worst case 10.9% to 38.0%. The target is 15% to 35%: FLAG.

50,000 bounded independent uniform component samples give mean 24.96% and 1st to 99th percentiles 15.81% to 33.74%. These distributions are assumptions, not measured process capability. Gap is shoulder height minus housing rim height. Its two height tolerances are sampled separately.

First-order variance contributors

The main contributor is the housing height limit. Tightening it or changing the gland design would need a new calculation and physical verification. The native model uses the nominal dimensions; this report does not establish a seal rating.

Thermal screen

With 1.5 W and assumed natural-convection coefficient 7 W/m²K, the single-node rise estimate is 10.9 K. This ignores internal hotspots and is not a measured temperature.
