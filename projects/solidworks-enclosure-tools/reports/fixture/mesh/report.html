Red: thin-wall samples. Orange: downward faces above bed. Hover for triangle ID. Both flags may apply. SolidWorks Student Edition exports. No physical printing or validation.

Wall rays sample face centroids, so small local defects can be missed. Bridge length is a downward-patch bounding span, not a verified bridge with anchored ends. Support is a vertical-column estimate sampled on a grid, not slicer output. Cost uses full-solid parts and effective support fill; time adds an assumed 30% travel allowance. Orientation ranking uses fit, support volume, then flagged face count. Use a slicer and print coupons before relying on this.
