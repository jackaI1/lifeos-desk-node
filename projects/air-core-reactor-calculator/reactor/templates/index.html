Before using a result

This is a geometry and circuit estimate, not a rated reactor design. It omits leads, helical pitch effects, nearby metal, stray capacitance, temperature rise, insulation ratings, and fault forces. The filament wire term assumes uniform low-frequency current. No physical measurements have been made.
